1. Schedule Confirmatory Diagnostic Labs

Machine learning screenings highlight probability patterns. Request formal diagnostic evaluations from a healthcare professional:

  • Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG) test
  • Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) Glycated Blood Test
  • Comprehensive Metabolic Panel & Lipid profile

2. Targeted Glycemic Nutrition

Clinical evidence supports stabilizing blood sugar through intentional dietary structure:

  • Prioritize high-viscosity soluble dietary fiber (beans, oats, legumes)
  • Replace refined simple carbohydrates with low-GI complex grains
  • Minimize sugar-sweetened beverages and ultra-processed snacks

3. Physical Conditioning & Muscle Uptake

Skeletal muscle is the primary organ responsible for non-insulin dependent glucose clearance:

  • Target at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity weekly
  • Incorporate 2–3 weekly resistance training sessions
  • Engage in 10-minute post-meal light walking
