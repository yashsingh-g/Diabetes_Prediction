Diagnostic Biomarkers Guide

Detailed clinical rationale behind each physiological parameter evaluated by the ensemble model.

Plasma Glucose

Target: <140 mg/dL

Blood sugar concentration measured during a standard 2-hour oral glucose tolerance test. The single strongest individual predictor of metabolic dysfunction.

Serum Insulin

Target: 16–166 μU/mL

Pancreatic hormone regulation. Abnormally high values point to peripheral insulin resistance, while depleted levels may indicate beta-cell fatigue.

Body Mass Index (BMI)

Target: 18.5–24.9 kg/m²

Anthropometric metric linking body mass to height. Excess adiposity is a recognized driver of chronic low-grade inflammation and reduced insulin sensitivity.

Diastolic BP

Target: <80 mmHg

Arterial resting pressure. Hypertension and dysglycemia frequently co-occur as key components of metabolic syndrome.

Age

Scale: Adult 21+

Risk progressively climbs with advancing age due to cumulative beta-cell wear, gradual metabolic rate reduction, and changes in lean body mass.

Pedigree Function (DPF)

Index: 0.08–2.42

Genetic scoring model estimating familial predisposition based on ancestral occurrence and degree of biological relation.

Triceps Skinfold

Target: 10–50 mm

Direct measure of subcutaneous body fat calibrated via calibrated caliper, providing localized body composition information complementary to BMI.

Pregnancies

Count: 0–17

Gestational stress history. Multiple pregnancies associate with transient glycemic spikes and elevated lifetime risk of developing Type 2 Diabetes.
